The engine

What {{ brand }} does

A rulebook-driven compliance engine — the law encoded as verifiable checkpoints across notice, consent, cookies, children's data, rights, security, breach readiness, retention, processors, cross-border transfers, governance, and cloud & endpoint posture.

🔍

Automated scanning

We read your public websites, cloud accounts and endpoints the way a regulator would — consent banners, trackers, encryption, exposed ports, device posture — with read-only, consent-gated access. Automation first; your teams only confirm what machines can't see.

📋

Guided questionnaire

What can't be seen from outside, your teams declare through a short structured questionnaire, pre-mapped to the checkpoints.

🧾

Evidence for everything

Every checkpoint — compliant, gap or not-applicable — carries masked evidence with a tamper-evident hash. Ready for your board or the Data Protection Board.

📊

Board-ready reports

A branded compliance report with a score, executive summary, findings and recommendations — saved as PDF in one click.

🔁

Continuous watch

Re-assess on schedule; compare any two reports to see what improved, what regressed, and what's new.

⚖️

Evolves with the law

The rulebook is versioned. When MeitY updates the Rules, checkpoints update and anything newly required is flagged — nothing changes silently. Your compliance posture tracks the law, not a snapshot of it.

🔔

Alerts that matter

If something regresses, a new third party appears, or the rulebook changes — you hear about it, by portal and email.
