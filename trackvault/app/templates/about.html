About {{ brand }}

Compliance you can prove, not just claim

{{ brand }} exists because most organisations facing India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act don't know where they stand — and the deadline doesn't wait.

🎯

What we do

We assess organisations against every checkpoint of the DPDP Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules 2025 — combining automated scanning of websites, cloud accounts and endpoints with structured declarations from your teams. The result is a scored, evidence-backed report your board and your regulator can rely on.

🧭

How we work

An engagement team runs every assessment. Automation does the heavy lifting; a person reviews every finding before it reaches you.

🔍

What we are

An assessment and monitoring service. We identify gaps, attach evidence, and recommend remediation with a clear path to close them.

🤝

What we are not

We are not the gap fillers, and we are not a law firm. Remediation of your systems happens only where you explicitly grant consent, access and permission — and our reports inform your legal counsel, they don't replace it.

Our commitment on your data: assessment data stays in your encrypted workspace. Evidence excerpts are masked for personal data. Credentials are encrypted at rest. Access to your systems is read-only and consent-gated, always.
