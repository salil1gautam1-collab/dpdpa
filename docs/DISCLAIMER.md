# Disclaimer & Terms of Engagement

This text ships embedded in every generated report.

---

**Scope of this tool.** DPDPA Sentinel identifies potential compliance gaps
against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025,
and records evidence of what was observed. It produces recommendations; it does
not by itself make an organisation compliant, and it is **not legal advice**.
Interpretation of the law for your organisation rests with your legal counsel
and company secretary.

**We are not the gap-fillers by default.** Remediation of identified gaps is
the organisation's responsibility. Where this tool offers assistance in closing
a gap (consent registers, DSR workflow, notices, suppression signals), that
assistance runs **only if the organisation explicitly grants consent, access
and permissions** for the specific system involved, in writing.

**Scanning consent.** Automated scans are passive reads of publicly accessible
pages and are run only for organisations that have authorised the scan in
writing. No credentials are used, no forms are submitted, no vulnerability
exploitation is performed.

**Evidence handling.** Evidence excerpts are masked for personal data before
storage. Scan outputs are stored locally for the engaging organisation and are
not shared with any third party.

**No warranty of completeness.** Automated checks cover what is observable from
outside; internal controls are assessed from the organisation's own declarations
and evidence. A checkpoint marked Compliant reflects the evidence available at
scan time, not a guarantee against regulatory findings.
